The game

Farm. Cook. Survive — together.

Players share a kitchen and a dwindling hunger meter. You grow and gather ingredients across the farmland, move them through processing units, and cook before time runs out. It's fully co-operative: everyone wins or everyone goes hungry.

🌾 Gather

Work the farmland and shelter to collect ingredients across the network of nodes.

⚙️ Process

Route ingredients through the processing units — timing and order matter.

🚚 Transport

Move ingredients through the right routes — by foot, truck, rail or ferry.

🍲 Cook

Feed the kitchen as events and the hunger meter push back. Co-operate or lose together.

At the table

Every play-test reshapes the rules.

Friends, strangers and fellow designers have all sat down with it — on café tables, floors and studio desks. Each session surfaces what's confusing, what drags, and what makes people laugh. That feedback goes straight back into the next version.
